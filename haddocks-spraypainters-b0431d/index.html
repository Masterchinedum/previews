Bay 03 · Marine

Boats, finished and protected with Nyalic.

We use Nyalic as the clearcoat on our boats. It is a marine protective coating for boats of all construction, protecting your asset against corrosion, oxidation and discoloration.

Underneath it: PPG Delfleet Evolution, a premium fleet paint system known for chemical resistance, gloss and durability.

Authorised Nyalic applicatorNyalic protects painted finishes and alloy against corrosion and oxidation. Ask us whether it suits your boat.

Boats we paint

Boats we paint and have painted include Extreme, Surtees and White Pointer.

SeaDek marine

Closed-cell EVA non-skid for decks, platforms and steps. It cushions long days standing, cuts noise and protects the deck, with marine-grade 3M adhesive backing.

The Haddock story

From one man and a spray gun to Valley Road

1980

Dave Haddock sets up his one-man spraypainting business.

2006

Panelbeating starts alongside the paint work, kept low key for now.

2008

A big year: 120 trucks and 400 aluminium boats through the booths.

2009

The 20 × 18 m panelbeating shop opens on the premises.

Today

Dave’s son, Director Goose Haddock, runs the much-expanded family business.
