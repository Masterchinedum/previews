Meet Sue and Brian

Brian has been a qualified auto and marine upholsterer since 1991. He has a good eye for detail and concept. Plenty of customers have explained an idea to him and been amazed at the finished piece he and the team designed.

We understand the passion our customers have for their boats, cars and motorhomes, and we're happy to help with all your upholstery needs.

“From the cigarette burn in your backseat to a total redesign of your motor home.”Brian & Sue Stockdale
  1. 1991

    Qualified in Christchurch

    Brian starts as a qualified auto and marine upholsterer.

  2. February 2011

    The earthquake

    The Christchurch quake hits our premises, so we move to Ashburton.

  3. December 2012

    The fire

    We lose the Ashburton workshop in a fire, with all of our equipment.

  4. The next 12 months

    On the road

    We buy a truck and run a mobile service while we move again, this time to Race Course Hill in Darfield.

  5. Early 2014

    A brand new workshop

    Built at Race Course Hill, and fully up and running.
