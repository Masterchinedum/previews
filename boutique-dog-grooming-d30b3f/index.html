How a visit works

Drop off, sip, pick up a star

Your dog gets the full salon treatment. You get a little time to yourself in the beautiful inner-city suburb of Mount Eden.

“Why not leave your pet with us while you enjoy a coffee or a nice meal?”

  1. 1

    Drop off

    Bring your dog to 611 Mount Eden Road. Park free right outside the door, or in our own car park down the driveway at the back.

  2. 2

    Enjoy a coffee

    Head out for a coffee or a nice meal at one of the many superb local cafes and eateries on Mount Eden Road.

  3. 3

    Pick up a star

    Come back to a freshly groomed, happy dog who has been treated like a star.

While you’re out, your dog waits in a secure, bright room with a comfy bed.
