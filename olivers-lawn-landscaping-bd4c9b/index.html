Both

Specialist lawn & water work

Available on commercial sites and at home.

Bores & reticulation

Planned and drafted for the best coverage with minimal wastage. Bores are installed and maintained through trusted associates we’ve worked with for many years.

Coring

Mechanical aeration that removes plugs of thatch and fractures the soil crust beneath the lawn: better roots, better water intake, less water used.

Fertilising & mulching

We recommend fertilising at least three times a year. Mulch, supplied and installed, cuts evaporation and weeds.

From the depot

Advice we give our own clients.

More than three decades of Perth summers and winters, boiled down.

Before summer

Check and reset your reticulation

Have your system checked and reset for correct water coverage before the start of summer. It’s essential in our hot climate.

Year-round

Fertilise at least three times a year

It keeps the root system stimulated and the lawn thick enough that weeds can’t germinate.

Winter

Mow more, spray less

Rather than overusing chemicals on winter weeds, an extra two or three mows often controls the problem. It’s often cheaper and kinder to the environment.
