The paint shop

We have gone green!

We spray with the Glasurit 90 line, which we call the world's No. 1 waterborne paint system. Here's what that means for your car.

  • Better colour accuracy. Most cars are now sprayed in waterborne from the factory. Add over 15,000 colour swatches and a colour-finding spectrometer machine.
  • Safer for our workers. A cleaner system in the booth.
  • A controlled booth. A high quality, full downdraft heated booth, where we control heat and humidity so our qualified spraypainters get the job right.
  • Factory finish or show finish. So no one will notice any work was ever done, or the super silky glass finish a show car needs to stand out.
