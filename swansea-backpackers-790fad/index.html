Out and about

Your base for Freycinet & Wineglass Bay

Swansea sits on the shores of Great Oyster Bay, on Tasmania's East Coast touring route, right in the middle of the coast.

55 min

From Swansea to Freycinet National Park and Wineglass Bay. Coles Bay, the gateway to the park, is the same drive.

The famous Freycinet Peninsula, the Hazards and other national parks, with waterfalls and walks that will astound, are all within easy driving distance.

Where we are

Right next door to the Bark Mill & Tavern
