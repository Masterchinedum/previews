The oven

A unique piece of colonial history, still lit every baking day

The Ross Village Bakery is Ross’ original bakery, and has been baking on this site for over 100 years in a traditional semi-scotch brick “3 bag oven”.

Each morning the oven is taken to over 700°F. Pies, pastries, breads, buns and cakes go in as the temperature gradually falls, and on busy days it’s fired again in the late morning.

  • 300loaves the oven can bake at a time
  • 700°Fand more, at the start of each baking day
  • 1barrow load of firewood, on average, each baking day
  • 100+years of baking on this site

“Sometimes the old fashioned way of doing things really is still the best, and we believe this is true of bread.”

From the Oven

  • i.Sourdough breads

    Handcrafted, from unbleached flours and old-fashioned recipes. Many loaves are baked straight on the oven floor, for an all-round crust.

  • ii.Tasmanian scallop pies

    Among the savoury pastries, and baked in the same brick oven as its heat falls through the morning.

  • iii.Vanilla slice

    Tassie’s best, by the bakery’s own reckoning. A sweet counterpart to the pies.

  • iv.Sweet & savoury pastries

    Tempting pastries alongside pies, buns and cakes, all from the same wood-fired oven.

Between Launceston & Hobart

At the centre of the Heritage Highway

Ross is about an hour from Launceston and an hour and a half from Hobart. Cross its famous bridge and stroll elm-lined streets past old sandstone buildings from its days as one of Tasmania’s early garrison towns.

Travelling on a tight schedule? Take a break and have a coffee with us.
