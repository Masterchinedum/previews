Meet Adam

The upholsterer who comes to your door

Adam GoloskySole traderCertified upholstererMarine & automotive trimmer

Adam did a 4-year apprenticeship, then gained valuable experience working in Sydney, Colorado in the USA and London, England, before starting his own upholstery business in Brookvale in 1992.

In 2006 Adam and his wife moved to the South Coast and formed Shellharbour Upholstery. Adam continues servicing his Brookvale customers as well as his clientele within the Illawarra.

His many long-standing, satisfied customers have proved to be his best advertising.
