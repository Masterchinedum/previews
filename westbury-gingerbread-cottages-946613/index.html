Explore from Westbury

The perfect base for Northern Tasmania

The quaint little village of Westbury was established in the 1820s as a garrison town. It sits at the centre of Northern Tasmania, just 20 minutes from Launceston.

In the village

  • The Village Green & Town CommonStroll on “a little slice of old England”.
  • St Andrews ChurchOpposite the Village Green; the foundation stone was laid in 1836.
  • The Westbury MazeA traditional hedge maze of 3,000 bushes, with paths over a kilometre long.
  • Pearn’s Steam WorldOver 100 steam engines, from trains to agricultural machinery.
  • Fitzpatricks InnWestbury’s first hotel, opened in 1833.

Further afield

Westbury is the perfect base from which to explore Launceston, Devonport, the Tamar wineries, Cradle Mountain, the Mole Creek Caves and the Northwest.

  • Mole Creek Karst National ParkTake a guided tour of King Solomon’s Cave and marvel at the glow worms in Marakoopa Cave.
  • The Great Western TiersA World Heritage Area, popular for its walks, waterfalls, historic homes and gardens.
  • DeloraineA major arts and crafts centre, with galleries and antique shops.
  • The Cream of Tasmania tourBetween Deloraine and Devonport: The House of Anvers, Ashgrove Farm Cheese, Christmas Hills Raspberry Farm and The Honey Farm.
