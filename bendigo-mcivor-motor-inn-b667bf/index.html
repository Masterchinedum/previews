Explore Bendigo

Gold-rush streets, a short stroll away

“Bendigo has something for everyone.”

The motel sits on McIvor Road, about 600 m from the edge of the central business district and the city's tourist destinations. Only minutes away, according to the motel:

  • Lakes & parks with playgrounds and BBQs
  • Trotting & harness racing tracks
  • Local wineries
  • Sporting grounds
  • Shopping centres & supermarket
  • La Trobe University
  • Hospitals
Dinner? Just cross the road.

The McIvor sits opposite eateries and food outlets, including a bistro/pub. Bendigo itself has everything from budget pub meals and Thai to fashionable dining.
